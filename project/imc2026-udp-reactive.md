# Anonymized UDP Reactive Telescope Packet Captures

The following dataset is part of the work leading to our paper *"Understanding Different Perspectives: Analyzing UDP Scanning Through Reactive Telescopes"* appearing in the 2026 ACM Internet Measurement Conference (IMC).

The dataset contains anonymized traces of traffic targeting our Reactive Telescope deployment between March 29th and April 19th 2026.
