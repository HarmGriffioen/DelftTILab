I am a researcher at the European Space Agency and in the Cybersecurity group at Delft University of Technology. I am also part of the Delft Threat Intelligence Lab. My interests include 5G/6G Non-Terrestrial Networks security, Internet protocols security, measurements, and Internet-of-Things. I obtained my PhD at Sapienza University of Rome.

Besides my academic experience, I also have 15+ years of industry background spanning between system and network administrations, system integration, containerization technologies, and Cybersecurity consulting.
