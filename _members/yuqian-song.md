Yuqian is a PhD candidate in the Cybersecurity group at Delft University of Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Georgios Smaragdakis and Prof. Harm Griffioen, since August 2024. 

He earned a BSc in Applied Mathematics and an MSc in Computer Science, specializing in Cybersecurity, both from TU Delft. His research focuses on database attacks, malware analysis and network scanning.
